About Our Brand

Crafting Royal Elegance For Every Celebration

Welcome to SHYAM MORCHHADI GARMENTS, your trusted destination for premium ethnic wear sherwani fabrics in Surat. We specialize in luxurious wedding and ethnic fabric collections designed for modern royal fashion. From rich jacquard textures to elegant velvet and designer sherwani fabrics, every collection reflects style, tradition and craftsmanship.

Our mission is to provide high-quality ethnic wear fabrics that help designers, retailers and customers create unforgettable wedding and festive looks. With a passion for fashion and excellence, we continue to bring trending and premium collections inspired by Indian royal heritage.
